## Privacy Policy

Danil Reznichenko built the Hideout app as an open source app. This app is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is.

**Information Collection and Use**

Hideout stores its settings on your device. To check for stable releases, it periodically sends an unauthenticated request to GitHub's public Releases API. The request identifies the client as Hideout but does not include the installed version, preferences, or account credentials. GitHub may process connection information such as your IP address and request time under its [General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
